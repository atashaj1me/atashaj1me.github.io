The Political Economy of China's Contemporary Banking Sector Coursework for Chinese Political Economy, Fulbright University Vietnam · Spring 2026 · Coauthor(s): LE Thien Ha Ngan

In progress — last updated 2026-05.

The Chinese Communist Party (CCP) perceives finance, dominated by banking activities, as one of its strategic bases of political power. In this framework of Chinese party-state capitalism, the party-state encroaches its influence over China's banking sector by utilizing institutionalized controlling channels such as regulatory control, ownership control, and personnel control. These institutional instruments let the party-state hold absolute authority over banking operations, but also create structural conflicts. We note that these frictions are not systemic failures but an equilibrium outcome regarding power dynamics between the state versus market, intra-state, and intra-market that reflect on-going political relations given institutional features of an authoritarian regime.
